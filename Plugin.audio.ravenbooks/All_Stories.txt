Angel of Death
Story by Trevor Ambrose.

Chapter 1.

Elizabeth Shaw grew up in a house filled with dark secrets. Her father, a drunken abuser, started tormenting her when she was just three years old, and it continued until she was ten. One night, she was awakened by her mother's desperate cries and the sound of gunshots. When she entered her parents' room, she found them both dead. All Elizabeth felt was numbness and loneliness. What would become of her now?

Chapter 2.

Twenty years later, those haunting memories still linger. Elizabeth watches Jeff Foster, a businessman who repeatedly abuses his wife. He is the first on her list of men who have raped women and escaped justice. Others on her list include Richard Evans, a lawyer; Steve Miller, a photographer; James Franklin, the President; and David Bishop, the Chief of Police. All these men must die for their crimes.

Killing them with poison was easy. They never saw it coming. But Elizabeth didn't anticipate Ethan Williams, a young detective on his first case, to be so relentless. The faster she drove, the closer he got. In a desperate move, she crashed into a wall. Feeling cornered, she fled to the rooftop of a nearby building.

Chapter 3.

Ethan followed her, his heart pounding as he reached the edge of the roof. "Elizabeth, it doesn't have to end this way!" he shouted, trying to reason with her. But Elizabeth, consumed by her past and the weight of her actions, looked at him with hollow eyes.

As they stood on the rooftop, a shocking realization dawned on Ethan. He recognized a birthmark on Elizabeth's wrist, identical to his own. "Elizabeth, I'm your brother," he said, his voice trembling. "I was taken away after our parents died. I've been searching for you ever since."

Chapter 4.

Elizabeth's eyes widened in disbelief. The memories of her lost brother flooded back, and for a moment, she hesitated. But the years of pain and vengeance were too much to overcome. With a final, sorrowful glance, Elizabeth stepped off the edge. Ethan watched helplessly as she plunged to her death, the tragic end of a woman haunted by her demons and the brother who found her too late.



Beyond Darkness

In the quaint coastal town of Everwood, where the salty breeze carried whispers of forgotten tales, stood a lighthouse. Its white walls gleamed against the stormy skies, guiding ships safely to harbor. But beyond its beacon of light lay a secret, a darkness that clung to the very stones of the tower.

Susan Anderson, a widow with two young daughters, Belle and Bethany, had moved to Everwood seeking solace after her husband's tragic death. The lighthouse keeper's cottage became their refuge, and Susan found comfort in the rhythmic sweep of the lantern's glow.

Grandma Lily, Susan's mother, had once been the caretaker of the lighthouse. Her eyes held ancient secrets, and her gnarled hands traced patterns on the windowsill as she spoke of the past. Susan listened, wide-eyed, as Lily recounted the night when the sea spat out a wounded stranger, an enigmatic man with silver eyes and a hunger for the moon.

"His name was Elias," Lily whispered, her voice barely audible. "He was no ordinary man, Susan. He was a werewolf, a creature torn between human and beast. The lighthouse was his sanctuary, and I tended to his wounds."

Susan's heart raced. Werewolves? Vampires? She had dismissed such tales as folklore, but Lily's words carried weight. The lighthouse had witnessed their battles, the moonlit clashes that shook the cliffs and echoed through the waves.

The vampires, led by the charismatic Victor, sought dominion over Everwood. Their pale skin shimmered like moonlight, and their fangs hungered for blood. Elias, the lone werewolf, fought to protect the town. His silver eyes blazed with determination, and Susan wondered if he held the key to the lighthouse's secret.

As the war escalated, Susan's daughters grew curious. Belle, the older sister, wandered the cliffs, collecting shells and glimpsing shadows in the moon's glow. Bethany, with her wild imagination, drew pictures of fanged creatures and whispered secrets to the wind.

One stormy night, Susan followed Lily to the lighthouse's hidden chamber, a place forbidden to outsiders. There, etched into the stone floor, were symbols of an ancient pact. Lily confessed her role: the pact that bound Elias to the lighthouse, granting him immortality in exchange for protection.

"But there's more," Lily confessed, tears in her eyes. "Elias loved a vampire, a forbidden love that shattered the fragile peace. When Victor discovered their affair, he cursed the lighthouse. Its light would forever attract supernatural beings, ensuring their eternal struggle."

Susan's mind reeled. The lighthouse was a battleground, its beacon both salvation and damnation. She wondered about her own daughters. What fate awaited them in Everwood?

As the final battle loomed, Susan faced a choice: protect her family or unravel the curse. She sought Elias, who stood at the cliff's edge, silver eyes haunted. Together, they deciphered the symbols, whispered forgotten incantations, and shattered the pact.

The lighthouse trembled, its walls cracking. The moonlight waned, and Susan watched as Elias transformed, a man once more, free from the curse. But the sacrifice was great. Lily, her grandmother, faded into the shadows, her secrets buried with her.

Everwood changed. The vampires retreated, and the lighthouse's light dimmed. Susan held her daughters close, knowing they would grow up with tales of bravery and sacrifice. Elias vanished into the night, leaving only memories and the echo of his howl.

And so, the lighthouse stood, beyond darkness, its stones etched with love and loss. Susan vowed to protect her family, but she wondered if the lighthouse's secret would resurface, binding Belle and Bethany to a destiny they couldn't escape.

In Everwood, where moonlight kissed the waves, the lighthouse whispered its tale, a beacon of hope and a keeper of shadows.



High Prize

Young Matt Fuller witnessed the unthinkable: the murder of his mother by Senator James Cooper. She had stumbled upon his embezzlement plans involving the insurance company where she worked. The image of her lifeless body haunted Matt, etching a path of anger and vengeance in his heart.

Meanwhile, five miles away, another young soul seethed with rage. Jeff Daniels watched as Senator William Davison's car exploded, killing his parents. Tears blurred his vision, and he vowed to make Davison pay.

Chapter 2: Matt's Redemption

Years passed, and Matt found himself in Dr. Kevin Patterson's office, battling alcoholism and the ghosts of his past. Dr. Patterson listened patiently as Matt spilled his secrets: the guilt, the anger, and the thirst for justice.

Then the phone rang. Jeff Daniels, now three decades older, demanded answers. Had Matt revealed anything about his parents' demise? Matt hesitated, torn between loyalty and revenge.

Chapter 3: The Presidential Race

As fate would have it, both James Cooper and William Davison announced their presidential candidacies. Matt's past collided with the present. Jeff Daniels saw an opportunity, a chance to manipulate the very men who had destroyed their lives.

Together, Jeff and Matt hatched a plan. They would play both sides, pulling the strings behind the scenes. Matt's redemption lay in exposing the truth, but revenge fueled his every move.

Chapter 4: The High Prize

The nation watched as the election unfolded. Cooper and Davison sparred, unaware of the puppet masters pulling their strings. Matt's loyalties wavered, torn between justice and vengeance.

And so, the high prize awaited, a revelation that would shatter political careers, expose corruption, and rewrite history. Matt Fuller, once a broken man, now held the fate of a nation in his hands.

But would he choose redemption or revenge?



High Prize: The Revenge

Chapter 1: The Unlikely Tie

Matt Fuller was a man of simple desires: a cold beer, a warm barstool, and the next drink. His life revolved around the pursuit of that elusive buzz, and he cared little for anything else. But fate had other plans for him.

On the day of the presidential election, Matt stumbled into the polling station, bleary-eyed and disheveled. The lines were long, and he grumbled about wasting precious drinking time. When the poll worker handed him the ballot, he scoffed and tossed it aside.

"Voting? Nah, not my thing," he slurred. "I've got more important matters to attend to."

As the day wore on, news spread like wildfire: a tie! James Cooper, the incumbent president, and William Davison, his charismatic opponent, were deadlocked. The entire nation held its breath, waiting for a resolution.

Chapter 2: The Desperate Campaigns

When Cooper and Davison learned about Matt Fuller's refusal to vote, panic set in. They dispatched their campaign teams to find him. Matt, blissfully unaware of the chaos he'd caused, nursed his hangover at a seedy dive bar.

Cooper's team arrived first. They promised Matt anything: a lifetime supply of beer, a private bar in the White House, even a beer-themed national holiday. But Matt just laughed, swaying on his barstool.

"Beer? Nah, I need something bigger," he slurred. "Like, make both of you donate your campaign funds to charity. Yeah, that'll do."

Davison's team wasn't far behind. They offered tax breaks for the middle class, free healthcare, and a parade in his honor. Matt scratched his scruffy beard.

"Nice try," he hiccupped. "But how 'bout taxing the rich to give every citizen a million bucks? Now that's a deal!"

Chapter 3: The Con Man Revealed

As the negotiations escalated, a mysterious figure entered the scene: Jeff Daniels, a smooth-talking man in an expensive suit. He sidled up to Matt at the bar, whispering promises of wealth and power.

"Matt," Jeff said, "I can make you the richest man in the country. Just vote the way I tell you."

Matt squinted at him. "Who are you?"

"Doesn't matter," Jeff replied. "Just remember: money talks."

But Matt was no fool. He smelled a rat. He agreed to Jeff's terms, all the while plotting his own twist. When the tie-breaking moment arrived, Matt cast his vote: "None of the above."

Chapter 4: Chaos Unleashed

The nation erupted in chaos. Cooper was arrested for election fraud, and Davison, defeated and broken, took his own life. Matt watched the news from his barstool, sipping a lukewarm beer.

And Jeff Daniels? Turns out he was a con man, manipulating both candidates for his own gain. Matt chuckled. "Guess I'm not the only one who likes a good drink."

As the dust settled, Matt found himself in possession of the presidency. His first act? Declaring a national holiday: "Beer Appreciation Day." The country rejoiced, and Matt raised his glass.

And so, President Matt Fuller, alcoholic, accidental hero, and lover of cold brews, set sail on Jeff Daniels' yacht, bound for Mexico. As the sun dipped below the horizon, he grinned.

"Cheers," he said. "To the high prize."



The Fan

Matt Anderson, an unassuming basketball enthusiast, spent his evenings immersed in online debates about the game. His passion for the sport was genuine, but he was merely an average fan, no more, no less. Little did he know that a seemingly innocuous chat room argument would unravel his life.

Enter James Harmon, an obsessed fan with an unhealthy fixation on the game. James's devotion bordered on fanaticism, and he took every disagreement personally. When Matt dared to challenge James's opinions, it ignited a virtual feud that transcended mere basketball banter.

The exchanges escalated. Matt, growing weary of James's relentless pursuit, finally snapped. "Get a life, James!" he typed, hitting send without a second thought. Those four words would change everything.

James's obsession transformed into something darker. He began stalking Matt, calling him incessantly, lurking outside his workplace, and even infiltrating his personal life. Matt's bank account dwindled as James siphoned off funds, leaving him financially vulnerable.

One fateful night, Matt returned home to find his house ransacked. James had left a chilling message scrawled on the wall: "You can't escape me." Fear gnawed at Matt's sanity as he realized he was dealing with a relentless predator.

The stalking intensified. James's car followed Matt's every move, narrowly missing him on several occasions. Matt's once-ordinary life now resembled a suspense thriller, with James as the malevolent antagonist.

Desperation drove Matt to seek help. But James was always one step ahead, taunting him with cryptic messages and veiled threats. Matt's friends and family dismissed his fears, attributing them to stress or paranoia.

Then came the unthinkable. Matt woke up in darkness, disoriented and bound. The cold, damp air clung to his skin as he realized he was trapped in a dimly lit dungeon. James had kidnapped him, and Matt's world had shrunk to the confines of his cell.

Days blurred into weeks. Matt's captor reveled in tormenting him, feeding him scraps, denying him light, and whispering deranged promises of eternal captivity. Matt's spirit waned, and he wondered if he'd ever see daylight again.

But within the darkness, something shifted. Matt's survival instinct kicked in. He remembered the countless basketball games he'd watched, the players who fought against all odds. If they could rise from defeat, so could he.

Matt plotted his escape, biding his time until James grew complacent. One moonless night, he seized an opportunity, the rusty lock yielding to his desperate strength. He stumbled out into the night, gasping for freedom.

The police found Matt wandering the streets, emaciated and haunted. James was apprehended, his obsession now turned toward a prison cell. Matt's ordeal had changed him forever, but he emerged with a newfound resilience.

As the courtroom doors closed behind James, Matt whispered, "Get a life, indeed."

Their online exchanges quickly turned into late-night conversations, as they shared stories of their adventures and dreams for the future. Despite the miles between them, the connection they once shared seemed to reignite, burning brighter than ever before.

As weeks turned into months, Alex found himself unable to resist the pull of their rekindled friendship. He made the bold decision to return to their hometown, hoping to finally reunite with Emily after years apart. With each passing mile, his heart raced with anticipation, longing to see her once again.

And as he stepped off the train and into the familiar streets of their childhood, Alex's heart soared with joy at the thought of finally being reunited with Emily. Chapter 4: Denial and Regret

As he rushed to her side in the hospital, he was consumed by a sense of helplessness and despair, his heart breaking at the sight of her frail form lying unconscious in the hospital bed.

With their past behind them and their future stretched out before them, Alex and Emily set out on a new adventure together. Hand in hand, they embraced the challenges and joys that lay ahead, knowing that as long as they had each other, they could weather any storm.

Echoes at Night

Chapter 1: The Seaside Escape

Alex and Emily, childhood sweethearts, had always dreamed of a peaceful life by the sea. They found their perfect haven in a quaint townhouse overlooking crashing waves and moonlit shores. The salty breeze whispered promises of love and tranquility.

Chapter 2: Whispers in the Walls

As the sun dipped below the horizon, strange echoes filled the townhouse. Muffled voices, distant cries, and footsteps haunted their nights. Alex dismissed it as old pipes, but Emily sensed something more. She traced her fingers along the ancient wallpaper, feeling the secrets etched into its fibers.

Chapter 3: The Ghostly Ballroom

One moonless night, they followed the ethereal music to the attic. There, in a forgotten ballroom, they glimpsed a ghostly waltz. A couple twirled, their eyes hollow, their love eternal. Alex and Emily shivered. Their love was powerful, but was it enough to face the spirits?

Chapter 4: The Haunting Intensifies

The townhouse revealed its dark history. A shipwreck had claimed lives just off the coast, and the souls of lost sailors wandered the halls. Objects moved on their own, shadows danced, and the air grew colder. Alex and Emily clung to each other, determined to unravel the mystery.

Chapter 5: The Séance

Desperate for answers, they invited a medium. In the flickering candlelight, the medium channeled the spirits. The sailor's widow, betrayed and heartbroken, sought solace. The ballroom had been her refuge, and she yearned for release. Alex and Emily vowed to help her find peace.

Chapter 6: The Sacrifice

The spirits demanded a sacrifice, a token of love to break the curse. Alex and Emily scoured old letters, discovering the widow's lost locket. It held a strand of her husband's hair. With trembling hands, they placed it on the ballroom floor, whispering vows of eternal devotion.

Chapter 7: The Final Confrontation

The townhouse trembled. The widow's spirit materialized, her eyes filled with longing. She danced with her lost love, their steps growing lighter. But another presence emerged, the malevolent captain who'd caused the shipwreck. He sought to keep them trapped forever.

Chapter 8: Love's Triumph

Alex and Emily stood firm. Their love blazed like a lighthouse, banishing darkness. They wove a protective circle, chanting ancient words. The widow's spirit embraced her husband, and together, they faced the captain. Love clashed with hatred, and the townhouse shook.

In a burst of light, the captain dissipated. The widow's spirit smiled, her form fading. She whispered her gratitude, promising to watch over Alex and Emily. As dawn broke, the townhouse stood silent, its walls no longer echoing with sorrow.

Alex and Emily held hands, their love stronger than ever. They'd defeated evil, but their bond was their greatest weapon. The townhouse, once haunted, now cradled their love, a beacon against the night's echoes.



Whispers at Midnight

Chapter 1: The Invitation

Alex and Emily received the ornate invitation in an old-fashioned envelope, its edges yellowed with time. Jake's elegant cursive promised an unforgettable evening, a murder mystery game night at his sprawling mansion. The address was etched in gold ink: Ravenwood Manor.

Chapter 2: The Gathering

The grand entrance of Ravenwood loomed, its stone gargoyles watching as guests arrived. Jason, the sharp-witted lawyer, greeted them. Susan, his wife, clutched her pearls nervously. Becky and Paul, Jake's sister and brother-in-law, whispered secrets. Dan, the shrewd businessman, exchanged glances with his wife. And then there was Carrie, Jake's enigmatic girlfriend, her eyes like shadows.

Chapter 3: The First Murder

As the clock struck midnight, the lights flickered. Dan stumbled into the room, blood staining his crisp white shirt. His last breath whispered a name: "Jake." Panic rippled through the group. Was this part of the game? Or had real darkness descended upon Ravenwood?

Chapter 4: Secrets Unveiled

Alex and Emily explored the mansion. Hidden passages revealed themselves, underground tunnels that snaked through the earth. In Jake's study, they found old journals. Jake's first wife, Evelyn, had vanished mysteriously. The ink on the pages told of love turned to madness, of whispered promises and betrayal.

Chapter 5: Echoes of the Past

Becky confessed to Emily. Jake had inherited Ravenwood after Evelyn's death. The house held secrets, echoes of lost souls. Alex wondered: Was Jake haunted by guilt? Or something more sinister?

Chapter 6: The Second Murder

Paul's lifeless body lay in the conservatory. Poisoned wine stained his lips. Emily clung to Alex. Trust wavered. Was Jake the puppet master, pulling strings? Or was there a puppeteer beyond the veil?

Chapter 7: The Haunting

Carrie led them to the attic. There, a portrait of Evelyn hung, a woman with hollow eyes. "Jake loved her," Carrie whispered. "But love can twist into obsession." The attic floor creaked. Shadows danced. Evelyn's ghost materialized, her voice a chilling echo: "He killed me."

Chapter 8: Love's Trial

Alex confronted Jake. The truth spilled like blood. Evelyn had discovered Jake's dark secret, the tunnels, the hidden rooms. She'd threatened to expose him. Jake's love had curdled into rage. He'd silenced her forever.

Emily's eyes locked with Alex's. Their love was their weapon. They rallied the remaining guests. The tunnels led to Jake's hidden chamber, a shrine to Evelyn. There, they found her locket, the same one from Echoes. Inside, a strand of hair, their proof.

As dawn approached, they confronted Jake. His eyes mirrored madness. "I loved her," he confessed. "But the house demanded sacrifice." The walls trembled. Shadows whispered. Evelyn's ghost emerged, her hand outstretched. Jake dissolved into mist.

Ravenwood stood silent. The tunnels collapsed. The locket glowed, sealing the past. Alex and Emily held each other. Their love had unmasked a killer. The mansion, once dark, now cradled their victory.

And as the sun rose, they vowed: Love would echo louder than any secret.

Evelyn's ghost wandered Parkridge, her love twisted into rage. Shadows danced, and the walls wept. Jake, consumed by guilt, buried her locket, the same one that would later bind Alex and Emily's fate. The manor became a vessel for sorrow, its halls echoing with lost souls.

Chapter 9: The Threads Converge

As the murder game unfolded, Evelyn's laughter intertwined with Emily's fear. Alex traced the tunnels, sensing the house's hunger. The spirit whispered: "Jake." The locket burned, revealing the truth. Alex and Emily confronted Jake, their love a beacon against the night's echoes.

Chapter 10: Love's Redemption

Alex and Emily faced the abyss. Evelyn's spirit embraced Jake. The locket glowed, sealing their fate. Parkridge collapsed, the tunnels collapsing, the ghosts finding peace. Alex whispered to Emily: "Love echoes louder than curses." As dawn painted the sky, Parkridge cradled their victory. The manor, once haunted, now held their love, a beacon against the night's whispers.

Chapter 11: Everlasting Echoes

Alex and Emily, their love unbreakable, settled into Parkridge Manor. The ancient walls whispered their names, and the spirits watched over them. They explored the hidden corners, the nursery where children's laughter lingered, the attic where memories danced. The locket, passed down through generations, glowed with Evelyn's essence.

Chapter 12: The Circle Complete

The manor thrived, no longer hungry for sacrifice. Alex and Emily's laughter echoed through the halls. They raised their children, teaching them the stories: the haunting, the betrayal, and the redemption. As the sun dipped below the horizon, Evelyn's spirit appeared, her hollow eyes filled with gratitude. Love had conquered darkness, and Parkridge Manor stood as a testament, a place where echoes of love would resonate forever.
